Author's Note

Before you begin

This story uses text messages, emails, calls and a few other documents. Italic teal text with a dotted rule is a character's private thoughts. Italic text with a terracotta bar is something quoted or remembered aloud.

Thank you for reading.

Chapter One

Low Water

The harbour was empty by the time she arrived.

Mara reached the end of the pier just as the last ferry pulled away, its wake folding itself into the dark. She stood there longer than she needed to, hands deep in her coat pockets, listening to the boards creak under her weight.

She had told herself the walk would be quick. Ten minutes, in and out. Nobody has to know you came back. It was easy to think that when the town was a hundred kilometres behind her.

Her phone buzzed against her hip. She took it out and let the screen light her face.

He always says it like it's a joke. Like I wouldn't go in. Like I haven't been going in for fifteen years, every night, in every dream, with the door already open.

Smaller. Everything is smaller. Why doesn't that help?

Her mother's voice arrived the way it always did, uninvited and perfectly clear.

The sea gives back what it takes. It just never gives it back in the same shape.
