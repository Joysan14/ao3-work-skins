.do-pov · .do-tag

Maren

The lighthouse had been dark for eleven days before anyone thought to ask why. Maren climbed the last of the stairs with her coat still wet, one hand on the rail, the other holding a lantern she did not need. Salt had worked its way into everything: the stone, the brass, the pages of the logbook lying open on the desk.

She read it standing up. The handwriting was her father's, and then, partway down the page, it was not.

"You shouldn't be up here," said a voice behind her.

"Neither should you," Maren said, without turning around. "That's the first thing I've learned today."
